Questions

Good to know.

Is installation really included?

Yes, always. You bring us your controller and we fit the new shell for you. There is nothing for you to take apart.

How do I know which model I have?

Look at the small grey text on the back of your controller. Type the serial number into the model finder above, or send us a photo of it and we will check.

Why are some colours only on certain models?

The early controllers (BDM-010 and BDM-020) and the later ones (BDM-030, 040 and 050) are shaped slightly differently inside, so each shell is made for one group.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. You can pay upfront, or spread the cost weekly or monthly. Just ask when you order.

My controller has a problem after the fit. What now?

Open a repair ticket with the CS serial from your order and tell us what is wrong. We will be in touch to sort it out.
